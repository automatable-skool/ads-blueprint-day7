Build the ENTIRE account in one run. By now the pieces are proven one by one - this capstone chains them end to end and finishes with the analysis that most agencies never do: whether the account's size matches the budget's ability to actually test. Monthly budget: $ARGUMENTS (ask if not given - everything downstream depends on it).

**Read `references/scaling.md` FIRST** - the budget-fit math, the four-check gate and its evidence, what carries over from a proven template and what must be re-earned, the launch ramp and bench rules, when to raise budget and when not, and the rules at the bottom.

**Preflight (hard gates):** account exists (`/account-setup` done), API live (`code/test_connection.py`), `context/proof.md` + `context/business.md` filled (`/context-layer` - without it this builds generic filler), compliance file read. Log/resume via `scale-log.md` across sessions.

**Phase order is load-bearing.** Pages exist AND ARE PUBLISHED before the ads that point at them - `/publish` runs inside this chain, before `/write-ads`, so every ad is created carrying its real LIVE final URL. Never localhost, never a home-page placeholder that has to be re-pointed later, which is the single most common way an account goes live aimed at the wrong page. Sitelink pages exist before the sitelinks. Tracking is verified before anything is enabled. Do not reorder to "save time" - it only moves the failure later, where it costs more.

**Where the bench lives:** `/campaign-plan` built three ad groups (one live, two paused). Everything below those three exists ONLY in `keyword-list.md` until this command creates it - this is the first time the rest of the map touches the account.

**The template is the REAL ad group that already converts, not a markdown spec.** Before stamping anything, confirm which live ad group passed the four-check gate (search terms trimmed · ad test called · **10% of clicks became leads AND 8% of impressions became clicks, over at least 500 clicks - 5% and 6% for roofing and HVAC - at or under target cost per lead** · landing page test called) and read it back from the API. When the spec and the account disagree, the account wins. Never stamp an unproven template across the map.

**The chain (approval gate after each phase - show me the output, wait for my yes):**

1. **Keywords → architecture.** Run `/keywords` end to end including the STAG stages → `keyword-list.md` (research at the bottom, the account structure section at the top). ⛔ I approve the map.
2. **Campaigns.** `/campaign-plan` builds the structure via `code/build_campaigns.py` per `references/campaigns.md` - settings exact (Search only, Presence, manual bidding to start). Verify the build matches the map with `code/verify_skag.py`. Everything PAUSED.
3. **The site layer, before any asset points at it.** `/standard-pages`: `/thank-you` with the conversion snippet, the six sitelink targets (pricing, emergency, reviews, areas served, guarantee, book-now) and the legal three, deployed and **each URL fetched live**. This comes before the ads because sitelinks cannot be built against pages that do not exist, and `/landing-page`'s tracking gate cannot verify a conversion without a distinct thank-you URL. These six do not depend on ad copy, so nothing is lost by building them first.
4. **Landing pages - one per NEW ad group, built in THIS run.** `/landing-page` per ad group (or per campaign where ad groups genuinely share one intent): the H1 carries the ad group's promise from the map word for word, CRO blueprint structure, **the tracking layer wired (form → `/thank-you`, click-to-call, and the Google phone snippet that swaps the number for ad visitors)**, speed-to-lead wired, deployed and each URL fetched live. **Every ad group this run creates gets its page here - no stamped group ships on a home-page placeholder.** The placeholder fallback in `/write-ads` exists for a missing page discovered late, not as a way for this command to skip page building. This comes before the ads because **an RSA cannot be pushed without a final URL** - build ads first and they get a placeholder that has to be re-pointed later, which is how ads end up enabled against the wrong page.
5. **Publish - the pages go LIVE before any ad exists.** `/publish`: deploy the site, wire the custom domain, then fetch every page from phases 3 and 4 on the LIVE domain and confirm each returns 200. This sits before the ads on purpose (Jono, 2026-09-01, after a demo run created ads against a home-page placeholder): phase 6's ads are created carrying their real live final URLs, and phase 7's tracking gate needs a live site for a real test submit. The home-page-placeholder fallback in `/write-ads` does not exist inside this chain - a page that is not live means publish it, never point an ad at a placeholder.
6. **Ads + assets - the FULL `/write-ads` flow, per new ad group, no shortcuts.** For EVERY ad group this run creates: the full library first - **about 100 headlines and 24 descriptions per ad group**, written to the scout's gaps - then **all three gates** (the mechanical cull, would-they-click-ours-over-theirs against the swipe file's best three per angle, the variety floor). The 30-survivor floor holds per group: fewer than 30 headlines through gate 2 means write more and gate again, never ship the best of a bad set. Then 2 RSAs per ad group, **each carrying its real LIVE final URL from creation - the published domain, never localhost, never a placeholder** - then the **complete asset set built ON the ad group** via `code/push_assets.py` - sitelinks, callouts, snippets, call asset, lead form, business name, logo, messages per `references/ad-assets.md`. `/write-ads`' one-ad-group default does not shrink this phase - this command names every new group in scope and each one gets the whole flow, library and gates included. A stamped group with no library, no gates run, or no assets is not stamped. All claims from proof.md. PAUSED. ⛔ I review the ads. Read the ad and its page back side by side so I can see the message match.
7. **Tracking hooked up + the webhook VERIFIED, per new page.** `/landing-page`'s tracking gate runs on EVERY page this run built, not just the first: the Google tag on the page, form → `/thank-you`, website-call and calls-from-ads conversions, Enhanced Conversions on, autopilot still OFF, negatives attached. **And the webhook is verified by a real test lead, not assumed from a page loading:** send a test submit through each new page's form and confirm the lead actually ARRIVED in GHL at `LEAD_WEBHOOK_URL` - open the CRM or read the webhook response, name the contact record. Same for the lead form asset's webhook. A webhook that returns 200 to nobody is a lead path that fails silently, and it is the single most expensive thing to discover after ads are enabled. A page whose test lead did not land in the CRM is NOT verified - say which page, and nothing on it gets enabled until it is.

**8. The budget-fit analysis (the capstone's signature - "right-sized testing"):**
- The math, shown legibly: daily budget = monthly ÷ 30.4 · my budget ÷ estimated CPCs (from the Planner data) = clicks/month → an ad group needs roughly 100+ clicks to call an ad test and 500 clicks at a 10% lead rate and an 8% click rate to graduate (Jono's winning bar; Google's own tCPA floor is 30 conversions) - at 2026 home-service medians, about $3,000 a month buys one group's worth of verdict-grade data, which is why the launch is ONE group with the whole budget
- Since 17 August 2026 a budget-limited target-CPA campaign hits its target rather than beating it - the target is now the price you pay, so set it from the last 30 days and never aspirationally
- **Verdict: how many ad groups my budget can genuinely TEST at once.** Budget spread across everything = nothing ever concludes; that's the classic self-inflicted injury
- The verdict is INFORMATION, never an action: how many groups the budget can honestly test, with the promotion rule written down (an enabled group graduates or fails out → the next group is worth enabling). I decide what to flip on. **This command NEVER pauses an ad group, never "benches" one, and never treats the map as the authority on what runs** - `keyword-list.md`'s order is the default BUILD order, and that is its entire job. It sets what gets built first, not what stays paused. (Jono, 2026-09-01)
- If the budget can't honestly test even 3 groups, say so and show what budget could - never pretend testing is happening when the math says it isn't

**9. The handoff screen - every wire named, nothing buried.** The account tree as built (campaigns → ad groups → ads → pages, enabled vs paused exactly as the API reports it - status is READ from the account, never assigned by this run), plus every wire named, **one line each, never a table**:

- Ad → landing page · per ad group: the final URL, fetched live · status
- The 6 sitelinks · target URL and status for each
- Call asset · the number in use, or **PENDING - no GHL number**
- Lead form asset · webhook live, or **PENDING - no `LEAD_WEBHOOK_URL`**
- Form conversion · the `/thank-you` page fires it, and a **test submit was actually seen** in Google Ads
- Phone conversions · call reporting ON at account level · **calls from ads** and **website calls** (the number swaps to a Google forwarding number for ad visitors) both PRIMARY at the 1-second minimum (any connected call counts) · click-to-call taps SECONDARY · a **test call was actually seen**
- Enhanced Conversions · on, and the gclid rides along on every form payload into GHL
- Negatives · account-level list attached

**Anything PENDING is read out loud with what it costs**, never left for me to notice. A campaign enabled with the call asset pending is a trades account with no phone button - say it.

Then the budget math, what I flip on myself (nothing goes live without my click), and the line that comes next: "Traffic flows now. Run `/audit` monthly - it ranks every dollar of waste and tells you what to fix first."
